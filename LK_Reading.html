Evidence context and review questions

AI-assisted screening of citation context, study scope, and questions a technical reviewer may want to resolve before applying a source to Montana’s Koocanusa Watershed.

How to read this section: Smart Citation labels describe how available citing sentences were classified, not whether a paper is right, wrong, strong, or weak. Most sources below have too little classified coverage for a sentiment conclusion. Retrieval date: September 18, 2026.

DeForest et al. (2012)

Alternative egg/ovary threshold framework · 35 citing publications

30 classified statements 1 supporting · 1 contrasting · 28 mentioning
What it can help answer
How an alternative fish-egg species-sensitivity distribution was assembled for a Canadian tissue-based guideline.
Review questions
Does its threshold method, toxicity dataset, and Canadian guideline purpose transfer to the Montana standard?
Why a reviewer may want to look closer
  • Scite identified Thomas & Janz (2015) ↗ as a contrasting citation on coldwater sensitivity. It is included in the reading list as a scoped alternative interpretation—not a conclusion that DeForest is invalid.
  • Confirm the original paper’s funding and conflict disclosures before relying on it in a policy context.
  • Compare the framework directly with EPA and Montana methods rather than treating either benchmark as interchangeable.

Presser & Naftz (2020)

USGS ecosystem-scale model record · 9 citing publications

Thin coverage: 3 classified statements 0 supporting · 0 contrasting · 3 mentioning
What it can help answer
The modeled pathway and site-specific inputs underlying the Lake Koocanusa water-column derivation.
Review questions
Which endpoint, conversion factors, particulate-speciation inputs, depths, locations, and temporal windows are appropriate for a comparison?
Why a reviewer may want to look closer
  • “No contrasting” is not evidence of agreement; the classified sample is too small.
  • Separate modeled outcomes from subsequently measured tissue data.
  • Review the report’s stated uncertainty and the reservoir’s spatial and depth stratification before pooling data.

Storb et al. (2023)

Elk Valley loading trends into Koocanusa · 25 citing publications

17 classified statements 17 mentioning · no stance conclusion
What it can help answer
Long-term selenium, nitrate, and sulfate loading trends from the Elk River toward Koocanusa Reservoir.
Review questions
Is a water/load result being extended beyond its study design to fish tissue, biological effects, or the dissolved-water standard?
Why a reviewer may want to look closer
  • It is a source and transport study, not a fish-effects study.
  • Its largely mentioning citation profile shows use as trend context, not independent validation of every conclusion.
  • Keep total and dissolved selenium separate in any comparison with Montana’s dissolved standard.

Alternative interpretations and questions for review

This pathway records where a scientist may reasonably ask a different question, prefer a different method, or limit the inference from a study. It is not a finding that any paper is invalid.

Criterion or model method

Could the choice of tissue endpoint, threshold derivation, Kd, or site-specific input materially change an interpretation?

Species, tissue, and study design

Do the sampled species, tissue type, reproductive stage, season, stations, and exposure setting match the question being asked?

Competing ecological explanations

Could prey, hydrology, harvest, density dependence, or another stressor help explain a fish-population pattern?
