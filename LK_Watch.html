USGS project update links selenium, burbot, and food-web research

USGS scientist Matthew Falcy describes two Kootenai Tribe-supported graduate projects. Manuel Carballo is combining a spatiotemporal selenium analysis, burbot tissue data, and a controlled larval-burbot dietary exposure experiment. Nate Nadal is linking nutrient additions and mountain-whitefish diets to selenium exposure through macroinvertebrate prey.

Why it matters: The article identifies active work that may produce future theses, reports, datasets, or papers. It also provides a credible project trail for the unresolved Falcy/Carballo/Nadal citation, but it is a project-news article rather than a final technical report or peer-reviewed publication.

Confirmed official update · reviewed October 20, 2026

B.C. opens Phase 2 review of the Canadian Koocanusa selenium target

B.C. initiated the second Elk Valley Water Quality Plan amendment in September 2026. Phase 2 will review the 2 µg/L total-selenium target for the Canadian portion of Koocanusa Reservoir using science developed since 2014. The public process anticipates consultation in September 2027 and submission to the minister by December 6, 2027.

Two June 2026 ministry reports are now public. The conditions review evaluates 2015–2025 monitoring and reports recent annual mean concentrations at LK2 of 1.19 µg/L in 2024 and 1.01 µg/L in 2025. The modeling review says the 2021 draft model generally overpredicted measured fish tissue, particularly higher-trophic fish, and recommends site-specific fish trophic-transfer factors and a multiple-scenario distribution approach.

Why it matters: These reports directly update the evidence and modeling context for Koocanusa. Their Canadian 2 µg/L target is based on total selenium and should not be treated as directly equivalent to Montana’s 0.8 µg/L dissolved standard.
