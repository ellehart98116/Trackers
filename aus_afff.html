Defence PFAS Management Sites & AFFF Source Areas

Twenty-eight Defence sites from the compiled website inventory. Icons show the AFFF-related source areas named in each site's remediation and management detail. Select a marker or list entry for the site record.
Sources. All site text is taken from the compiled inventory of Australian Department of Defence PFAS management-site web pages; each record links to its source page. Coordinates are approximate base-centroid positions added for mapping only — they are not from the inventory and should be replaced with surveyed or cadastral coordinates before any use in a deliverable. Marker positions are offset from their true location where sites would overlap; the thin leader line points to the mapped position. Absence of an icon means no source area of that type was named in the inventory text — not that none exists. Verify all content against the source pages before use.
